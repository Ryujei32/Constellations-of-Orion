This is the Constellations of Orion, a very small mod that adds stuff to the game

Expect pretty cool star like stuff

Currently a beta of the mod, for now the mod only adds

16 Items
1 NPC
3 Tiles
1 Beta pet
1 Beta whip

To craft the items you will need the Constellite bars, the ore will Spawn after defeating the Wall of Flash


I will add more content to this, any feedback and ideas you guys have just leave on the comments
